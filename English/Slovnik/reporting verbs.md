# reporting verbs

**Úvodní slovesa – slovesa, kterými uvozujeme nepřímou řeč (nejen say a tell).**

> He suggested going by train. / He promised to come home early.

---

## Podrobně

### Přehled sloves

> accused, admitted, advised, agreed, apologised, begged, boasted, complained, confessed, explained, invited, offered, ordered, promised, replied, suggested, thought

> He invited me to the party. / He begged me to come to the party. / He ordered me to come to the party.

### Tři vazby

**1) sloveso + that**

said, told, pointed out, explained, wrote, complained, agreed, claim, repeat, mention, insist, state

*That* se v hovorové angličtině často **vynechává**.

> She answered (that) she had never wanted to study biology.

**2) sloveso + infinitiv** – když má hlavní i vedlejší věta **stejný podmět**

admitted, agreed, confessed, offered, ordered, promised, suggested, refused

> He promised to come home early.

**3) sloveso + předmět + infinitiv**

asked, begged, warned, wanted, advised, promised, offered, threatened, encouraged, persuaded, reminded, assured

> He promised us to come home early.

### Vazby s předložkou

| Sloveso | Vazba | Příklad |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| warn | **against** + -ing | warned us **against** swimming |
| suggest | **+ -ing** | suggested **going** |
| remind | **to** + infinitiv | reminded us **to phone** |
| insist | **on** + -ing | insist **on paying** |
| accuse | **of** + -ing | accused me **of** copying |
| apologise | **for** + -ing | apologised **for** not going |
