# impersonal passive

**Neosobní trpný rod – formální způsob, jak říct „říká se, že…", „věří se, že…".**

> It is believed that climate change is accelerating.

---

## Podrobně

### Dvě konstrukce

**1) It + be + believed/said/thought…** (věty se dvěma podměty)

> They expected that the hurricane would lose strength gradually.
>
> → **It was expected that** the hurricane would lose strength gradually.

Používá se **impersonal IT** a je to **formální angličtina** – v běžné konverzaci s kamarády se nepoužívá.

**2) subject + be + believed/known… + to + infinitiv** (věty s jedním podmětem)

> The number of extreme weather events **is expected to increase**. – o přítomnosti

> **subject + be + believed… + to + have + past participle**

> Alexander the Great **is known to have died** in Babylon. – o minulosti

### Slovesa, která se takhle používají

say, think, believe, consider, know, suppose, expect, claim, allege, report, prove

> He is said to be very smart.
>
> The politician is alleged to have accepted a bribe.
>
> Seven people were reported to have died.

### Pomůcka

- děj **teď** → *to + infinitiv* (is thought to be)
- děj **dřív** → *to have + příčestí* (is believed to have spread)
