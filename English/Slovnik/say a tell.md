# say a tell

**Say i tell znamenají říct, ale po tell se hned uvádí komu.**

> She told me that she would call. / She said that it was raining.

---

## Podrobně

### Rozdíl

| Sloveso | Co po něm následuje | Příklad |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **tell** | **ihned osoba** (me, you, him, her, us, them) | She **told me** that she would call at 2pm. |
| **say** | **that**, nebo komu s předložkou **to** | She **said that** it was raining. / She **said to us** that it was raining. |

### Časté chyby

❌ She said me that… → ✅ She **told** me that…

❌ She told that… → ✅ She **said** that… / She told **me** that…

### Příklady

> He **told** everybody that he had to do it.
>
> Alice **told** us that she was going to bed.
>
> Jordan **said** that he was tired.
>
> "I've already seen this film", Mark **said to** me.
