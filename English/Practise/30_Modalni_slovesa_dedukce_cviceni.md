# Cvičení – Modální slovesa pro dedukci

Zápis: [[30_Modalni_slovesa_dedukce]]

## Vyber správnou reakci

> A: My great-grandmother had five houses.
>
> a) Really? She must be rich.
> b) Really? She must have been rich.

## Translate

1. It was put into the wardrobe. Who could have put it there?
2. It could have been worse.
3. She can't have been bored by the lecture, so she must have been tired.
4. They can't have walked all the way here, so they must have taken a bus.
5. Alan seems to know everything. He must have studied hard.
6. Sandra seems to be sad. She may have lost something.
7. He looks like he might have lost quite a bit of weigh.
8. They must have seen you coming home.
9. She must have forgotten about it.

## Doplňte – modals in the past

1. She's very angry with me. I ............... something wrong. (must, do)
2. I ............... too much salt in the soup. Does it taste good? (may, put)
3. Joseph ............... that. He would never be rude like this. (can't, say)
4. What a surprise! You ............... us you were coming. (might, tell)
5. It is a shame the class finished so early. We ............... another exercise. (could, do)
6. Sally didn't say hello to me the other day. – She ............... you. (may, not, notice)
7. John left the cinema halfway through the film. He ............... it. (might, not, like)

## Doplňte must / can't (be)

1. The baby has just had his bottle of milk. He .......... be hungry!
2. You have lived here for so many years. You ......... know a lot of people!
3. He has been on duty all night. He ........... be exhausted.
4. She always makes people repeat. She .......... be a bit deaf.
5. I wonder who is talking behind the door. – It ........ be Doris, she ......... working now.
6. He knows a lot about biology. He ..... be fond of the subject.
7. The people next door ...... having a party. The music is so loud.
8. It has been raining for weeks. The tourists ........ enjoying their time!

## Výsledky

### Vyber správnou reakci

**b)** Really? She must **have been** rich. (mluvíme o minulosti)

### Translate

1. Bylo to dáno do skříně. Kdo to tam jen mohl dát?
2. Mohlo to být horší.
3. Nemohla být znuděna přednáškou, takže musela být unavená.
4. Určitě nešli celou cestu pěšky tady, takže museli jet autobusem.
5. Zdá se, že Alan ví všechno. Určitě pilně studoval.
6. Zdá se, že je Sandra smutná. Možná něco ztratila.
7. Vypadá, jakoby ztratil hodně na váze.
8. Určitě tě viděli, jak jdeš domů.
9. Určitě na to zapoměla.

### Modals in the past

1. I **must have done** something wrong.
2. I **may have put** too much salt in the soup.
3. Joseph **can't have said** that.
4. You **might have told** us you were coming.
5. We **could have done** another exercise.
6. She **may not have noticed** you.
7. He **might not have liked** it.

### must / can't (be)

1. He **can't be** hungry!
2. You **must know** a lot of people!
3. He **must be** exhausted.
4. She **must be** a bit deaf.
5. It **can't be** Doris, she **must be** working now.
6. He **must be** fond of the subject.
7. The people next door **must be** having a party.
8. The tourists **can't be** enjoying their time!
