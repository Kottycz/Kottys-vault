# Cvičení – Stavová a dynamická slovesa

Zápis: [[02_Stavova_a_dynamicka_slovesa]]

## Doplň správný tvar

1. The cake they baked yesterday ____________ (taste) amazing.
2. It ____________ (sound) like the neighbors are having a party.
3. I ____________ (smell) something delicious from the kitchen.
4. The ocean ____________ (look) stunning at sunset.
5. The new car interior ____________ (feel) very luxurious.
6. She ____________ (seem) quite tired after the long trip.
7. They ____________ (taste) the new dish for the first time right now.
8. The music in the background ____________ (sound) very soothing.
9. This sweater ____________ (feel) really soft against my skin.
10. I can't join you for dinner right now; I ____________ (smell) the flowers in the garden.
11. They ____________ (look) at the stars through a telescope.
12. Lately, she ____________ (seem) happier than usual.
13. I ___________ (feel) a bit tired after a long day at work.

## Translate

1. I feel I should go on holiday.
2. How are you feeling today?
3. Justin enjoys skiing.
4. He was enjoying himself at the party.
5. She looks lovely.
6. He is looking at this picture.
7. He appears to be very proud of you.
8. Tom Cruise is appearing in the movie next week.

## Výsledky

### Doplň správný tvar

1. The cake they baked yesterday **tastes** amazing.
2. It **sounds** like the neighbors are having a party.
3. I **am smelling** something delicious from the kitchen.
4. The ocean **looks** stunning at sunset.
5. The new car interior **feels** very luxurious.
6. She **seems** quite tired after the long trip.
7. They **are tasting** the new dish for the first time.
8. The music in the background **sounds** very soothing.
9. This sweater **feels** really soft against my skin.
10. I can't join you for dinner right now; I **am smelling** the flowers in the garden.
11. They **are looking** at the stars through a telescope.
12. Lately, she **seems** happier than usual.
13. I **am feeling** a bit tired after a long day at work.

### Translate

1. Mám pocit, že bych měl/a jet na dovolenou.
2. Jak se dnes cítíš?
3. Justin má rád lyžování.
4. Bavil se na večírku.
5. Ona vypadá krásně.
6. On se dívá na tuto fotku.
7. Zdá se, že je na tebe velmi hrdý.
8. Tom Cruise se objeví ve filmu příští týden.
