# Cvičení – Nepřímá řeč

Zápis: [[16_Neprima_rec]]

## Převeďte do nepřímé řeči

1. Emily: "Our teacher will go to Rome tomorrow."
2. Helen: "I was writing a letter yesterday."
3. Robert: "My brother created a computer virus last year."
4. Lisa: "Tim went to the stadium an hour ago."
5. Patricia: "Will your mother celebrate her birthday next weekend?"
6. Jason and Victoria: "We will do our best in the exams today."
7. Alice: "The robbers are robbing the bank now!"
8. The criminal: "I commited the crime here."

## Převeďte věty

1. "I am going crazy" she said.
2. "There is a nice weather"
3. "We lived in Spain"
4. "She has moved away."
5. "Rob will play with you".
6. "The students can/may travel too".

## Výsledky

### Převod do nepřímé řeči

1. Emily said that their teacher **would go** to Rome **the next/following day**.
2. Helen said that she'**d been writing** a letter **the day before**.
3. Robert boasted that his brother **had created** a computer virus **the year before**.
4. Lisa said that Tim'**d gone** to the stadium **an hour before**.
5. Patricia asked me **if** my mother **would celebrate** her birthday **the following weekend**.
6. Jason and Victoria promised that they **would do** their best in the exams **that day**.
7. Alice warned him that the robbers **were robbing** the bank **then**.
8. The criminal admited that he **had commited** the crime **there**.

### Převod vět

1. She said that she **was going** crazy.
2. They told us there **was** a nice weather.
3. He said they **had lived** in Spain.
4. She said she **had moved** away.
5. She said Rob **would play** with me.
6. She said that the students **could/might** travel too.
