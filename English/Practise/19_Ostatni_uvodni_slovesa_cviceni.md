# Cvičení – Ostatní úvodní slovesa

Zápis: [[19_Ostatni_uvodni_slovesa]]

## Say or tell?

1. He ..... everybody that he had to do it.
2. Alice ..... us that she was going to bed.
3. "We had never paid a fine for speeding", they ..... to us.
4. Jordan ...... that he was tired.
5. "I've already seen this film", Mark ......to me.
6. "This joke made me laugh", Susan ..... to me.

## Přelož slovíčka a použij ve větě

declare, explain, inform, insist, mention, persuade, reassure, suggest, admit, advise, announce, assure, claim, complain, confirm, convince

## Translate the sentences

1. "Yes, you're right, it's a terrible problem."
2. "I'm sorry I didn't get to the meeting."
3. "They didn't do their job properly."
4. "Sorry I'm late. The traffic was bad and then I couldn't find a parking space."
5. "Those bags must be heavy, John. Shall I take one?"
6. "Yes, honest, I'll be there on time. I won't be late."

## Translate z češtiny

1. Řekl mi, abych to nedělal.
2. Poradil jim, aby šli rychle pryč.
3. Povzbudil mě, abych se nebál.
4. Přesvědčila ho, aby tam jel.
5. Připomněli mi, abych odevzdala tu práci.
6. Chtěl po mě, abych se více učil.
7. Požádali jsme je, aby nám o tom vyprávěli.

## Choose the correct words to complete the sentences

1. A man on the beach warned us ___ swimming in the sea because it was very stormy.
   A against B for
2. Roberta suggested ___ to the cinema on Saturday.
   A to go B going
3. Dad reminded us ___ him when we arrived in London.
   A phoning B to phone
4. I insist on ___ for this pizza. You bought me lunch last week.
   A paying B to pay
5. Gary accused me ___ copying his homework.
   A for B of

## Výsledky

### Say or tell?

1. He **told** everybody that he had to do it.
2. Alice **told** us that she was going to bed.
3. "We had never paid a fine for speeding", they **said** to us.
4. Jordan **said** that he was tired.
5. "I've already seen this film", Mark **said** to me.
6. "This joke made me laugh", Susan **said** to me.

### Translate the sentences

1. She agreed that it was a terrible problem.
2. He apologised for not going to the meeting.
3. She complained that they hadn't done their job properly.
4. He explained that the traffic had been bad.
5. She offered to carry a bag for him.
6. He promised that he would be there on time.

### Translate z češtiny

1. He told me not to do it.
2. He advised them to go away quickly.
3. He encouraged me not to be afraid.
4. She persuaded him to go there.
5. They reminded me to hand in the work.
6. He wanted me to learn more.
7. We asked them to tell us about it.

### Choose the correct words

1 **A** (warned us against)
2 **B** (suggested going)
3 **B** (reminded us to phone)
4 **A** (insist on paying)
5 **B** (accused me of)
