# Cvičení – Modální slovesa (přítomnost)

Zápis: [[08_Modalni_slovesa_pritomnost]]

## Doplň modální slovesa, která vyjadřují

> had better (not), must, needn't, have to, don't have to, can, can't, mustn't, may, (not) be allowed to, don't need to, should, shouldn't, ought to

- povinnost: ....................
- zákaz: ....................
- absenci povinnosti: ....................
- svolení: ....................
- radu: ....................

## Practise – must / mustn't / needn't / may

1. I haven't got lots of money; I .......... waste it.
2. Don't worry Paul, you've got plenty of time, you ...... hurry.
3. Do you want me to wait for you? No it's OK, you ...... wait.
4. What kind of pet do you want to buy? Well, it ....... be very sweet with my baby.
5. You ....... phone me when you arrive at home but I hope you will.
6. Davis is asleep, you ...... shout like that.
7. Peter ...... smoke at home but only in the garden.
8. You can tell Fiona what I said but she ...... tell her mum.
9. My friend gave me a present for my brother, I ........ forget to give him.
10. It surprises me that you ....... watch violent films.

## must or have to?

1. She's a really nice person. You ______ meet her.
2. You can't turn right here. You _____ turn left.
3. My eyesight isn't very good. I _____ wear glasses for reading.
4. I _____ get up early tomorrow. I'm going away and my train leaves at 7:30.
5. In Britain many children ___ wear uniform when they go to school.
6. When you come to London again, you ___ come and see us.

## Which one is correct?

1. In tennis you don't have to / can't touch the net.
2. Students can't / have to stand when a teacher comes into the room.
3. We don't have to / doesn't have to go to school at the weekend.
4. Teachers don't have to / can't be late for classes.
5. Basketball players may / have to be tall.

## Doplňování

1. In Britain many children ___ wear uniform when they go to school.
2. When you come to London again, you ___ come and see us.
3. Last night Don became ill suddenly. We ___ call a doctor.
4. You really ____ work harder if you want to pass the examination.
5. I'm afraid I can't come tomorrow. I ____ work late.
6. I'm sorry I couldn't come yesterday. I ___ work late.
7. Paul doesn't like his new job. Sometimes he ___ work at weekends.

## Výsledky

### Doplň modální slovesa

- **povinnost:** must, have to
- **zákaz:** mustn't, can't, not be allowed to
- **absenci povinnosti:** needn't, don't have to, don't need to
- **svolení:** may, can, be allowed to
- **radu:** should, shouldn't, had better (not), ought to, need to

### must / mustn't / needn't / may

1. I haven't got lots of money; I **mustn't** waste it.
2. Don't worry Paul, you've got plenty of time, you **needn't** hurry.
3. Do you want me to wait for you? No it's OK, you **needn't** wait.
4. What kind of pet do you want to buy? Well, it **must** be very sweet with my baby.
5. You **needn't** phone me when you arrive at home but I hope you will.
6. Davis is asleep, you **mustn't** shout like that.
7. Peter **may** smoke at home but only in the garden.
8. You can tell Fiona what I said but she **mustn't** tell her mum.
9. My friend gave me a present for my brother, I **mustn't** forget to give him.
10. It surprises me that you **may** watch violent films.

### must or have to?

1. She's a really nice person. You **must** meet her.
2. You can't turn right here. You **have to** turn left.
3. My eyesight isn't very good. I **have to** wear glasses for reading.
4. I **must/have to** get up early tomorrow. I'm going away and my train leaves at 7:30.
5. In Britain many children **have to** wear uniform when they go to school.
6. When you come to London again, you **must** come and see us.

### Which one is correct?

1. In tennis you **can't** touch the net.
2. Students **have to** stand when a teacher comes into the room.
3. We **don't have to** go to school at the weekend.
4. Teachers **can't** be late for classes.
5. Basketball players **have to** be tall.

### Doplňování

1. In Britain many children **have to** wear uniform when they go to school.
2. When you come to London again, you **must** come and see us.
3. Last night Don became ill suddenly. We **had to** call a doctor.
4. You really **must** work harder if you want to pass the examination.
5. I'm afraid I can't come tomorrow. I **have to** work late.
6. I'm sorry I couldn't come yesterday. I **had to** work late.
7. Paul doesn't like his new job. Sometimes he **has to** work at weekends.
