# Cvičení – Nepřímé otázky

Zápis: [[18_Neprime_otazky]]

## Report the questions

1. Are Uncle Matt and his girlfriend getting married in July?
   We asked our mum ________________________________________ .
2. What songs are your band performing at the concert on Friday?
   I asked Darren ________________________________________ .
3. Did you buy anything in town?
   Mandy asked me ________________________________________ .
4. Have you ever tried rock climbing?
   The boys asked us ________________________________________ .
5. Who's won the school science competition?
   We asked the teacher ________________________________________ .

## Yes/no questions

1. "Do you know London?"
2. "Did he leave for work?"
3. "Have you been to Madagaskar?"
4. "Can you help me?"
5. "Are you working tonight?"

## "wh" questions

1. "Where is Sam?"
2. "What were you doing?"
3. "Why did you stay at home last night?"
4. "Who wants to play soccer?"
5. "How is your mother?"

## Výsledky

### Report the questions

1. We asked our mum **if/whether Uncle Matt and his girlfriend were getting married in July**.
2. I asked Darren **what songs his band were performing at the concert on Friday**.
3. Mandy asked me **if/whether I had bought anything in town**.
4. The boys asked us **if/whether we had ever tried rock climbing**.
5. We asked the teacher **who had won the school science competition**.

### Yes/no questions

1. She asked me if I knew London.
2. He asked me if he had left for work.
3. They questioned me if I had been to Madagascar.
4. She asked me if I could help her.
5. She asked me if I was working that night.

### "wh" questions

1. She asked me where Sam was.
2. She asked me what I had been doing.
3. She wanted to know why I had stayed at home the night before.
4. He wondered who wanted to play soccer.
5. They asked me how my mother was.
