# Cvičení – Some x Any

Zápis: [[20_Some_x_any]]

## Complete the sentences with some, any or a/an

1. There isn't ________ sugar left.
2. Are there ________ good restaurants in your town?
3. Would you like dessert? There's ________ ice cream in the freezer.
4. Look! There are ________ children playing on the beach.
5. I can't see ________ litter in the park. It's a very clean place.
6. She has ________ nice car.
7. Did you get ________ bread this morning?
8. I've made ________ sandwiches for you.
9. Have you got ________ answer from the office?
10. She didn't get ________ e-mails yesterday.
11. There isn't ________ swimming pool.

---

## Výsledky

1. There isn't **any** sugar left.
2. Are there **any** good restaurants in your town?
3. Would you like dessert? There's **some** ice cream in the freezer.
4. Look! There are **some** children playing on the beach.
5. I can't see **any** litter in the park. It's a very clean place.
6. She has **a** nice car.
7. Did you get **any** bread this morning?
8. I've made **some** sandwiches for you.
9. Have you got **an** answer from the office?
10. She didn't get **any** e-mails yesterday.
11. There isn't **a** swimming pool.
