# Cvičení – used to, would, past simple

Zápis: [[04_Used_to_a_would]]

## USED TO, WOULD, PAST SIMPLE

1. My family ……… (live) in a flat in east London.
2. They ………. (go) shopping in the market together.
3. We ………… (help) in the factory for three years.
4. Last week they ……… (spend) a nice time in the mountains.
5. When they ……. (move) to the house they ……..(visit) her very often.
6. She …………(be) lonely because she lived on her own.
7. They ………. (win) the lottery three times.
8. They …… (not eat) any healthy food and they still don't eat it.
9. Did you .....(sleep) well last night?
10. I ....... sometimes (drive) to work.

---

## Výsledky

1. My family **used to live** in a flat in east London.
2. They **would go** shopping in the market together.
3. We **would help** in the factory for three years.
4. Last week they **spent** a nice time in the mountains.
5. When they **moved** to the house they **would visit** her very often.
6. She **used to be** lonely because she lived on her own.
7. They **won** the lottery three times.
8. They **didn't eat** any healthy food and they still don't eat it.
9. Did you **sleep** well last night?
10. I **would** sometimes **drive** to work.
