# Cvičení – Impersonal passive constructions

Zápis: [[15_Impersonal_passive]]

## Rewrite the sentences using the impersonal IT construction

1. People claim that the new policy will benefit small businesses.
2. Experts said that that medicine was effective for treating headaches.
3. They have reported that the train has been delayed due to technical issues.
4. Scientists believe that climate change is accelerating.
5. Journalists think that the new film will break box office records.

## Rewrite the sentences using subject + be + believed/known, etc. + to + infinitive/past participle

1. The police think the suspect is hiding in a remote area.
2. Researchers believe that the virus spread faster than expected.
3. They believe the team will win the championship.
4. Historians believe that the manuscript was written in the 12th century.
5. Scientists claim that the dinosaurs became extinct due to a meteorite.
6. Analysts think the company is planning a major merger.

## Výsledky

### Impersonal IT construction

1. It is claimed that the new policy will benefit small businesses.
2. It was said that that medicine was effective for treating headaches.
3. It has been reported that the train has been delayed due to technical issues.
4. It is believed that climate change is accelerating.
5. It is thought that the new film will break box office records.

### subject + be + believed/known, etc.

1. The suspect is thought to be hiding in a remote area.
2. The virus is believed to have spread faster than expected.
3. The team is believed to win the championship.
4. The manuscript is believed to have been written in the 12th century.
5. The dinosaurs are claimed to have become extinct due to a meteorite.
6. The company is thought to be planning a major merger.
