# Cvičení – Budoucí průběhový a předbudoucí čas

Zápis: [[07_Budouci_prubehovy_a_predbudouci_cas]]

## Change the verb into the correct form

1. I .... (send) the project by Friday.
2. We .... (reach) the top before noon.
3. Morgan .... (sell) his car by next Sunday.
4. In 2032, they .... (work) here for 20 years.

## Which one is correct?

Antony dreams about his future. – This time next year:

1. I'll be having / I'll have a nice car.
2. I'll be preparing / I'll prepare for my holiday in Havaii.
3. I won't be learning / I won't learn at school.
4. I'll be living / I'll live in a beautiful house.
5. A lot of money will be belonging / will belong to me.
6. I'll be knowing / I'll know a lot of celebrities.

## Which is correct, a or b?

1. If he continues drinking so fast, he ____ the whole bottle by midnight.
   a) will have drunk b) will be drinking
2. By the time we get there, the fitness centre ____
   a) will close b) will have closed
3. Tomorrow at 3 p.m. they ____ from Madrid to Prague.
   a) will have travelled b) will be travelling
4. After I finish reading this book, I ____ all the books by this author.
   a) will read b) will have read
