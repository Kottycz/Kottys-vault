# Cvičení – MAKE OR DO?

Zápis: [[01_Make_or_do]]

## Match – do / make

**do:** without, damage, crossword puzzles, business

**make:** speech, fun, sure, plans

## Complete – make/do in the correct form

1. The house was a disaster. They ______ a terrible mess.
2. Have you ______ all the arrangements yet?
3. Vincent: English football fans came to Valencia last weekend. Maria: Did they _____ any damage?
4. Listen. I made lunch so you can ______ the washing up. It's only fair.
5. He was about to ______ his excuses when Mr Halloran asked him a peculiar question.
6. They're ______ plans for their wedding.
7. Who's going to ______ dinner? You or me?
8. Be careful that you ______ the right decision.
9. He's _____ an appointment to see the dentist on Thursday.
10. Would you ______ me a favour and feed the cat while I'm away.
11. We normally _____ the shopping on Saturday mornings.
12. Before you go out, you have to _____ your homework, OK?
13. This cake is delicious. Did you really _____ it?

## Translate – get

1. I can definitely tell you're getting better.
2. It's getting hotter.
3. By the time they reached the house they were getting hungry.
4. He got rich soon after he started his business.
5. My mother's getting old and needs looking after.
6. It gets dark very early in the winter.
7. Don't touch the stove until is gets cool.

## Výsledky

### Complete – make/do

1. They **made** a terrible mess.
2. Have you **made** all the arrangements yet?
3. Did they **do** any damage?
4. …so you can **do** the washing up.
5. He was about to **make** his excuses…
6. They're **making** plans for their wedding.
7. Who's going to **make** dinner?
8. Be careful that you **make** the right decision.
9. He's **made** an appointment to see the dentist on Thursday.
10. Would you **do** me a favour…
11. We normally **do** the shopping on Saturday mornings.
12. …you have to **do** your homework, OK?
13. Did you really **make** it?

*(Pravidlo: **do** = vykonávat činnost, **make** = něco vytvořit.)*

### Translate – get

1. Rozhodně poznám, že se zlepšuješ.
2. Otepluje se.
3. Než došli k domu, začínali mít hlad.
4. Zbohatl brzy poté, co začal podnikat.
5. Moje máma stárne a potřebuje péči.
6. V zimě se stmívá velmi brzy.
7. Nedotýkej se sporáku, dokud nevychladne.
