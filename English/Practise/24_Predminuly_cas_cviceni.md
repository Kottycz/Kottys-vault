# Cvičení – Předminulý čas

Zápis: [[24_Predminuly_cas]]

## What is the difference?

1. When Jane arrived home, the children were eating dinner.
2. When Jane arrived home, the children ate dinner.
3. When Jane arrived home, the children had eaten dinner.

## Join the sentences using the words in brackets and the Past Perfect Simple

1. We played cards. We went to sleep. (before)
2. The children broke the cups. They hid them. (after)
3. He read the story to children. He liked it when he was young. (because)
4. She burned the dinner. They went to the restaurant. (when)

## Výsledky

### What is the difference?

1. Když Jane přišla domů, děti **právě večeřely** (děj probíhal).
2. Když Jane přišla domů, děti **se navečeřely** (děj následoval po jejím příchodu).
3. Když Jane přišla domů, děti **už byly po večeři** (děj skončil před jejím příchodem).

### Join the sentences

1. We **had played** cards before we went to sleep.
2. After the children **had broken** the cups, they hid them.
3. He read the story to children, because he **had liked** it when he was young.
4. When she **had burned** the dinner, they went to the restaurant.
