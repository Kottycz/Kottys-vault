# Cvičení – SO x SUCH

Zápis: [[27_So_x_such]]

## Fill in – so, such (a/an)

1. His exam was ... disappointment that I couldn't believe it.
2. We really liked his first girlfriend. She was … nice.
3. They spent … nice day on bike.
4. Who could have written … impolite letter?
5. That's a bad boy. .... rude!

## Doplň so x such (a/an)

1. He owned ________ many books that his walls were lined with bookcases.
2. The boys were _______ excited to sit still.
3. They have ________ a few enemies, they are accepted wherever they go.
4. The snow was ________ deep for us to walk across the field.
5. Yesterday I walked ________ far that I fell asleep immediately after supper.
6. I had ________ good time at the party, I did not want to leave.
7. I see her ________ often that I feel I know her quite well.
8. This is ________ interesting book, I stayed up all night to read it.
9. This puzzle is ________ easy that a child could do it.
10. There was ________ much traffic, I could not cross the street.
11. She was ________ tired to watch the video.

---

## Výsledky

### Fill in

1. His exam was **such a** disappointment that I couldn't believe it.
2. We really liked his first girlfriend. She was **so** nice.
3. They spent **such a** nice day on bike.
4. Who could have written **such an** impolite letter?
5. That's a bad boy. **So** rude!
