## Rewrite the story using who, which, that, where or and

> I was sitting in a café. I often go there for a drink after work. I called the waiter. I know him quite well. I asked for a cup of coffee.
>
> While I was waiting I looked at a newspaper. It was lying on the table. I started reading an article on the front page. It said: 'Police are looking for a woman, Catherine. She has been missing from her home for two weeks.'

## Výsledky

### Rewrite the story

> I was sitting in a café **where** I often go for a drink after work. I called the waiter **who** I know quite well **and** I asked for a cup of coffee. While I was waiting I looked at a newspaper **which** was lying on the table **and** I started reading an article on the front page. It said that the police are looking for a woman, Catherine **who** has been missing from her home for two weeks. I looked at the photo **and** it showed a woman with dark curly hair, she had a round face. I recognized at once **that** she was my new neighbor **who** had moved in just 2 weeks ago.

### Lze vztažné zájmeno vynechat?

Pravidlo: vynechat jde **jen tehdy, když zájmeno nenahrazuje podmět** a věta **není** neurčující (oddělená čárkami).

- **Nelze vynechat** (zájmeno je podmět): 1, 5, 13
- **Nelze vynechat** (neurčující věta s čárkami): 2, 11
- **Lze vynechat:** 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12
