# Cvičení – Podmínkové věty (0, 1, 2)

Zápis: [[11_Podminkove_vety]]

## Zero conditional

1. If people ...... (eat) too much, they .... (get) fat.
2. If you.... (touch) a fire, you ..... (get) burned.
3. People.... (die) if they ..... (not eat).
4. If she ..... (study) hard, she always ..... (pass) her exams.
5. You .... (get) water if you ..... (mix) hydrogen and oxygen.
6. Snakes...... (bite) if they ..... (be) scared.
7. Unless he .... (ask), I never .... (answer) his questions.
8. If babies ...... (be) hungry, they .... (cry).
9. The grass .... (not grow) well unless it .... (rain) properly.

## First conditional – dopis

> Hi brother,
>
> We're at the station, but our train isn't here yet. We're worried because if the train ___1___ (not arrive) on time, we ___2___ (miss) our flight, and we ___3___ (have to) spend the night here. When we ___4___ (get) to the airport, I ___5___ (text) you.
>
> We are very excited about the holiday. Sandy is looking forward to taking surfing lessons. She says as soon as we ___6___ (check in) at the hotel, she ___7___ (look for) a surf instructor. However, she says she won't take any lessons if I ___8___ (not surf) with her.
>
> We expect the weather will be fine. If it ___9___ (not be), we ___10___ (be) very upset. We are also planning to try the local food. If the hotel restaurant ___11___ (be) nice, we ___12___ (have) a meal there as soon as we ___13___ (check in). One more thing, ___14___ (you/water) the plants if I ___15___ (promise) to take you out for dinner when we come back?
>
> Take care,
>
> Andy.

## First conditional – věty

1. If the bus is late, we (walk).
2. She will call them if she (have) time.
3. If it costs too much, I (buy) a smaller one.
4. If the flight for New York is full, I (go) somewhere else.
5. What will we do if the taxi (not come)?
6. Will you phone me if there (be) any problems?
7. I (ask) Anthony if I see him tomorrow.

## Translate – zero, first or second conditional?

1. If you had the chance to play any sport for your national team, which sport would you play?
2. Půjdeš, jestli budeš pozván do kina dnes večer?
3. Když upustíš vajíčko, rozbije se.
4. Co děláváš, když tě bolí hlava?
5. Jestli bude venku zima, vezmu si kabát.
6. Zasnoubil by se s ní, kdyby měla více peněz?

## Výsledky

### Zero conditional

1. If people **eat** too much, they **get** fat.
2. If you **touch** a fire, you **get** burned.
3. People **die** if they **don't eat**.
4. If she **studies** hard, she always **passes** her exams.
5. You **get** water if you **mix** hydrogen and oxygen.
6. Snakes **bite** if they **are** scared.
7. Unless he **asks**, I never **answer** his questions.
8. If babies **are** hungry, they **cry**.
9. The grass **doesn't grow** well unless it **rains** properly.

### First conditional – dopis

> We're worried because if the train **doesn't arrive** on time, we **will miss** our flight, and we **will have to** spend the night here. When we **get** to the airport, I **will text** you.
>
> She says as soon as we **check in** at the hotel, she **will look for** a surf instructor. However, she says she won't take any lessons if I **don't surf** with her.
>
> If it **isn't**, we **will be** very upset. If the hotel restaurant **is** nice, we **will have** a meal there as soon as we **check in**. One more thing, **will you water** the plants if I **promise** to take you out for dinner when we come back?

### First conditional – věty

1. If the bus is late, we **will walk**.
2. She will call them if she **has** time.
3. If it costs too much, I **will buy** a smaller one.
4. If the flight for New York is full, I **will go** somewhere else.
5. What will we do if the taxi **doesn't come**?
6. Will you phone me if there **are** any problems?
7. I **will ask** Anthony if I see him tomorrow.

### Translate

1. Kdybys měl šanci hrát jakýkoli sport za tvůj národní tým, který sport bys hrál?
2. Will you go if you are invited to the cinema tonight?
3. If you drop an egg, it shatters.
4. What do you do if you have a headache.
5. If there is cold outside I'll get my coat.
6. Would he get engage to her if she had more money?
