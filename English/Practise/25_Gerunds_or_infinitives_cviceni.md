# Cvičení – Gerunds or Infinitives

Zápis: [[25_Gerunds_or_infinitives]]

## Playing or To play? Doplň sloveso v infinitivu nebo -ing tvaru

1. I'm interested in __________ (learn) about different cultures.
2. After school, we often go __________ (run) in the park.
3. __________ (solve) complex math problems is not always easy.
4. Sarah avoids __________ (go) to bed late.
5. She appeared __________ (to be) very tired after the long journey.
6. It's important __________ (study) regularly to pass the exam.
7. She was kind enough __________ (help) us with our project.
8. He is too tired __________ (drive) all the way to the city.

---

## Výsledky

1. I'm interested in **learning** about different cultures.
2. After school, we often go **running** in the park.
3. **Solving** complex math problems is not always easy.
4. Sarah avoids **going** to bed late.
5. She appeared **to be** very tired after the long journey.
6. It's important **to study** regularly to pass the exam.
7. She was kind enough **to help** us with our project.
8. He is too tired **to drive** all the way to the city.
