# Cvičení – Neurčitá zájmena

Zápis: [[26_Neurcita_zajmena]]

## Translate

1. Anyone can see what's going on here.
2. Everyone clapped when the movie ended.
3. Does anyone have something that could help me with anything?
4. Everything is taken care of.
5. I really just needed some time away from everyone.
6. My parents always told me that anything is possible.
7. I would live anywhere hot. It doesn't matter.
8. He just needs somewhere to stay.

## Put in indefinite pronouns

1. I didn't see Max at the gym. In fact, I didn't see ..... The place was empty.
2. James, ..... is here to see you.
3. .... can play that game. It is very easy.
4. Peter didn't see .... suspicious.
5. Davis told ..... his secret. He is a mystery man.
6. I have .... for you. Look.

## Výsledky

### Translate

1. Kdokoli může vidět, oč tu běží.
2. Každý zatleskal, když film skončil.
3. Má někdo něco, co by mi pomohlo s čímkoli?
4. O vše je postaráno.
5. Já opravdu jen potřebuji nějaký čas být pryč ode všech.
6. Mí rodiče mi vždy říkali, že cokoli je možné.
7. Žila bych kdekoliv, kde je horko. Je mi to jedno.
8. Jen potřebuje někde žít.

### Put in indefinite pronouns

1. I didn't see **anybody**. The place was empty.
2. James, **somebody** is here to see you.
3. **Anybody** can play that game. It is very easy.
4. Peter didn't see **anything** suspicious.
5. Davis told **nobody** his secret. He is a mystery man.
6. I have **something** for you. Look.
