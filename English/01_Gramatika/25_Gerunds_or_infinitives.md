# 25 – Gerunds or Infinitives (verb patterns)

Verb Pattern Unit 9E – Playing or To play?

## Verb + -ing form

Po těchto slovesech následuje sloveso s koncovkou **-ing**:

**1)** admit, avoid, can't help, can't stand, consider, don't mind, deny, encourage, enjoy, fancy, feel like, finish, insist on, imagine, miss, practise, keep, postpone, recommend, spend time, quit, suggest

> They insist on not sending the email.

**2)** Po **předložkách a frázových slovesech** – good at playing, give up smoking, go on reading

## Verb + infinitive

Po těchto slovesech následuje **infinitiv s to**:

**1)** afford, agree, appear, ask, decide, expect, fail, forget, hesitate, hope, learn, manage, mean, need, offer, plan, prepare, pretend, promise, refuse, seem, want, wish, would like, intend

> They agreed not to send that email.

**2)** Po **přídavných jménech** – easy to find, difficult to find, funny to do …

**3)** Po výrazech **enough/too** – strong enough to lift …/ too cold to go out..

## help

Po slovesu **'help'** můžete použít **'to' nebo ne** – oba způsoby jsou správné. Obecně platí, že forma **bez 'to' je častější**.

> He helped me carry the boxes.
>
> He helped me to carry the boxes.

> Reading before bed helps me relax.
>
> Reading before bed helps me to relax.

## make

Po slovesu **'make'** používáme **infinitiv bez 'to'**.

**MAKE** = Vynutit nebo vyžadovat od někoho, aby vykonal určitou akci.

> After Billy broke the neighbor's window, his parents made him pay for it.

## Verb + infinitive or -ing form

Po těchto slovesech následuje **infinitiv nebo -ingový tvar** (beze změny významu):

> begin, continue, hate, like, love, prefer, start

> Example: It started raining = It started to rain

### Řešení – Playing or To play?

1. I'm interested in **learning** about different cultures.
2. After school, we often go **running** in the park.
3. **Solving** complex math problems is not always easy.
4. Sarah avoids **going** to bed late.
5. She appeared **to be** very tired after the long journey.
6. It's important **to study** regularly to pass the exam.
7. She was kind enough **to help** us with our project.
8. He is too tired **to drive** all the way to the city.
