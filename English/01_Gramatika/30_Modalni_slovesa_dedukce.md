# 30 – Modální slovesa pro dedukci (probability)

## Základní pravidlo

Pro vyjádření **jistoty / určité míry pravděpodobnosti** použijeme **must / can't**, jestliže si **nejsme jisti**, můžeme použít slovesa **may, might, could, should** a **shouldn't**.

## Vyber správnou reakci

> A: My great-grandmother had five houses.
>
> B: Really? She must **be** rich.
>
> B: Really? She must **have been** rich. ✔

## Slovesa pro minulost

Pro stupeň jistoty vyjadřující **minulost** používáme tato slovesa:

| Sloveso | Význam | Příklad |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **can't have** | přece/určitě ne | He can't have done it. |
| **could have** | mohl | Who could have done it? |
| **may (not) have** | možná (ne...) | He may (not) have done it. |
| **might (not) have** | možná/snad (ne..) | He might (not) have done it. |
| **must have** | určitě | He must have done it. |
| **couldn't have** | nemohl | You couldn't have seen Louis in the town. |

Pokud chceme **kritizovat již proběhlé děje**:

**should / shouldn't have** – měl/neměl

> You should have phoned her before you went out.

## Tři časové roviny

Stupeň jistoty vyjadřujeme pro **přítomnost, budoucnost a minulost**.

**1) Přítomnost**

> She is a great pianist. She must play the piano (every day).
>
> Listen! Somebody must be playing the piano (now).

**2) Budoucnost**

> We may play tennis tomorrow.

**3) Minulost**

> They may have played tennis last week too.

## Modal verbs of probability in present

**Where is Olivia?**

> She **must** be on the bus.
>
> She **might** come soon.
>
> She **could** be lost.
>
> She **may** be in the wrong room.
>
> She **can't** be at home.
>
> She **should** be at hospital.
>
> She **shouldn't** be at work.

## Modal verbs of probability in the past

**Where was Olivia last night?**

> **Podmět + must/might … + have + ….-ed / 3. sl.**

> She **must have forgotten** about our date.
>
> She **might have worked** late.
>
> She **could have taken** the wrong bus.
>
> She **may have felt** ill.
>
> She **can't have stayed** at home.

## Negative

> **MODAL + NOT + BASE VERB + PAST PARTICIPLE** (to show how certain the speaker is that something **didn't** happen)

- May not have done
- Might not have done

**Can't or couldn't have + past participle** shows that the speaker is **very certain** something didn't happen.

> Robin looks tired today. She can't have slept well last night.

## Srovnejte

| Where is Olivia **now**? | Where was Olivia **yesterday**? |
| :-- | :-- |
| She must be on the bus. | She must have forgotten about our meeting. |
| She might be driving to school. | She might have worked late. |
| She could be lost. | She could have taken the wrong bus. |

### Translate – řešení

1. It was put into the wardrobe. Who could have put it there? → Bylo to dáno do skříně. Kdo to tam jen mohl dát?
2. It could have been worse. → Mohlo to být horší.
3. She can't have been bored by the lecture, so she must have been tired. → Nemohla být znuděna přednáškou, takže musela být unavená.
4. They can't have walked all the way here, so they must have taken a bus. → Určitě nešli celou cestu pěšky tady, takže museli jet autobusem.
5. Alan seems to know everything. He must have studied hard. → Zdá se, že Alan ví všechno. Určitě pilně studoval.
6. Sandra seems to be sad. She may have lost something. → Zdá se, že je Sandra smutná. Možná něco ztratila.
7. He looks like he might have lost quite a bit of weigh. → Vypadá, jakoby ztratil hodně na váze.
8. They must have seen you coming home. → Určitě tě viděli, jak jdeš domů.
9. She must have forgotten about it. → Určitě na to zapoměla.

### Key – modals in the past

1. She's very angry with me. I **must have done** something wrong.
2. I **may have put** too much salt in the soup. Does it taste good?
3. Joseph **can't have said** that. He would never be rude like this.
4. What a surprise! You **might have told** us you were coming.
5. It is a shame the class finished so early. We **could have done** another exercise.
6. Sally didn't say hello to me the other day. – She **may not have noticed** you.
7. John left the cinema halfway through the film. He **might not have liked** it.

### Results – must / can't (be)

1. The baby has just had his bottle of milk. He **can't be** hungry!
2. You have lived here for so many years. You **must know** a lot of people!
3. He has been on duty all night. He **must be** exhausted.
4. She always makes people repeat. She **must be** a bit deaf.
5. I wonder who is talking behind the door. – It **can't be** Doris, she **must be** working now.
6. He knows a lot about biology. He **must be** fond of the subject.
7. The people next door **must be** having a party. The music is so loud.
8. It has been raining for weeks. The tourists **can't be** enjoying their time!
