# 17 – Nepřímá řeč: rozkazy a návrhy

## Reported commands (nepřímé rozkazy a zákazy)

### Přímá řeč → nepřímá řeč

**Imperative (rozkaz a zákaz)**

> "Close the door." ➡ He told me **to close** the door.

**Negative imperative**

> "Don't be late." ➡ She told us **not to be** late.

### Slovesa používaná pro reported commands

- **tell** – rozkaz, autorita
- **ask** – slušná žádost
- (méně často: **order, warn, remind, advise**)

> The teacher said: "Don't use your phones." ➡ The teacher told us not to use our phones.

## Reported suggestions (nepřímé návrhy)

### Nejčastější struktury

**a) suggest + gerund (-ing)**

> "Let's go by train." ➡ She suggested **going** by train.

**b) suggest (that) + subject + should / bare infinitive**

> "You should see a doctor." ➡ He suggested (that) I (should) see a doctor.

(**should** se často vynechává – obě varianty jsou správné)

## Přehled

| Přímá řeč | Nepřímá řeč |
| :-- | :-- |
| "Sit down." | He told me to sit down. |
| "Don't touch it." | She told him not to touch it. |
| "Let's leave early." | They suggested leaving early. |
| "You should study more." | He suggested (that) I (should) study more. |

## Typické chyby studentů

❌ He suggested me to go.
✅ He suggested going / that I go.

❌ She told not to be late.
✅ She told us not to be late.
