# 27 – SO x SUCH

## so, such – tak/takový

**So + přídavné jméno/příslovce**

> It's so cold.
>
> They all ran so quickly.

**Such + (a/an) + přídavné jméno s podstatným jm.**

> She's such a nice person.

## Cvičení

Zadání: [[27_So_x_such_cviceni]]

### Solution – Fill in so, such (a/an)

1. His exam was **such a** disappointment that I couldn't believe it.
2. We really liked his first girlfriend. She was **so** nice.
3. They spent **such a** nice day on bike.
4. Who could have written **such an** impolite letter?
5. That's a bad boy. **So** rude!

### Solution – Doplň so x such (a/an)

1. He owned **so** many books that his walls were lined with bookcases.
2. The boys were **so** excited to sit still.
3. They have **such a** few enemies, they are accepted wherever they go.
4. The snow was **so** deep for us to walk across the field.
5. Yesterday I walked **so** far that I fell asleep immediately after supper.
6. I had **such a** good time at the party, I did not want to leave.
7. I see her **so** often that I feel I know her quite well.
8. This is **such an** interesting book, I stayed up all night to read it.
9. This puzzle is **so** easy that a child could do it.
10. There was **so** much traffic, I could not cross the street.
11. She was **so** tired to watch the video.
