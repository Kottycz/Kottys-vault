# 14 – Trpný rod

## Passive tenses

| Čas | Příklad |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Present Simple** | The newspaper **is published** daily. / The newspapers **are published** daily. |
| **Past Simple** | This house **was built** in 1850. / These houses **were built** in 1850. |
| **Present Perfect** | Our car **has been stolen**. / Our cars **have been stolen**. |
| **Future Simple** | The car **will be returned** to you the same day. / The cars **will be returned** to you the same day. |
| **Past Perfect** | His money **had been stolen** before he returned home. |

## Převod z činného do trpného rodu

| Činný rod | Trpný rod |
| :-- | :-- |
| LED uses no mercury and lead. | No mercury and lead is used in LED. |
| Now, he is using the second hand electronic devices. | Now, the second hand electronic devices are being used by him. |
| They have updated the database security settings. | The database security settings have been updated. |
| In the 1970's people started to use green and yellow LEDs. | Green and yellow LEDs were used in the 1970's. |
| Engineers might develop AI algorithms for better predictions. | AI algorithms might be developed by engineers for better predictions. |
| Electricians will use tools like pliers, wrenches and srewdrives. | Tools like pliers, wrenches and srewdrives will be used by electricians. |
| They have used special electrical istallation components. | The special electrical istallation components have been used. |
| They would use five sockets outlets in the kitchen. | The five sockets outlets would be used in the kitchen. |

## Passive voice – simple x continuous

I v trpném rodě rozlišujeme **průběhovou a neprůběhovou formu**. Průběhové formy se v anglickém jazyce užívá jen u času **přítomného a minulého**.

> A new bridge **is being built** in our street.
>
> V naší ulici se staví nový most. (Stavba mostu probíhá).

> A new bridge **is built** in our street.
>
> V naší ulici je postaven nový most. (Most je hotový, stavba skončila).

## Kdy použít trpný rod

Trpný rod spíše než činný použijeme tehdy, kdy **činitel děje není důležitý**, v daný okamžik **není potřeba ho zmiňovat**, nebo **činitele děje prostě neznáme**.

> We were served in the restaurant quickly. (by waiters)
>
> My car was stolen yesterday. (Who stole the car?)
>
> America was discovered in 1492. (The date is more important than by whom.)

Někdy je **slušnější** použít trpný rod než činný:

> My room hasn't been cleaned yet.
>
> You haven't cleaned my room yet.

## by, with

Původce děje se ve větě s trpným rodem uvádí předložkou **by (osoba)**, **with (nástroj)**. Je-li původce děje neurčitý, může se **vynechat**.

> We were invited to a party **by** Peter.
>
> Football is played all over the world. (by people)
>
> The door was opened **with** a card not a key.
>
> First, cake is cut (with a knife) and then served.

## Remember

1. **The present perfect continuous, the future continuous, the past perfect continuous, and the future perfect continuous are not normally used in the passive.**
2. We can use the verb **to get** instead of the verb **to be** in everyday speech when we talk about things that happen **by accident or unexpectedly**.

   > Four people **got** hurt in the car crash.

   However, we **cannot use get to describe states**.

   > That house **is** (NOT gets) owned by my uncle.
