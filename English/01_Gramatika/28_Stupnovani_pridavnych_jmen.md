# 28 – Stupňování přídavných jmen

## Tvoření stupňů

**Krátká přídavná jména:**

> small – smaller – the smallest
>
> nice – nicer – the nicest
>
> hot – hotter – the hottest
>
> lazy – lazier – the laziest

**Dlouhá přídavná jména:**

> interesting – more interesting – the most interesting
>
> helpful – more helpful – the most helpful

**Nepravidelná přídavná jména:**

> good – better – the best
>
> bad – worse – the worst
>
> little – less – the least
>
> much/many – more – the most
>
> far – farther – the farthest
>
> far – further – the furthest

## Nestupňovatelná přídavná jména

Existují i přídavná jména tzv. **nestupňovatelná**, kde by stupňování nedávalo smysl, např.:

- roční (annual)
- mrtvý (dead)
- neznámý (unknown)
- ležící (lying)

## Dvě možné formy

Some adjectives have **two possible forms** of comparison (-er/est and more/most).

| positive | comparative | superlative |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| clever | cleverer / more clever | cleverest / most clever |
| common | commoner / more common | commonest / most common |
| likely | likelier / more likely | likeliest / most likely |
| pleasant | pleasanter / more pleasant | pleasantest / most pleasant |
| polite | politer / more polite | politest / most polite |
| quiet | quieter / more quiet | quietest / most quiet |
| simple | simpler / more simple | simplest / most simple |
| stupid | stupider / more stupid | stupidest / most stupid |
| subtle | subtler / more subtle | subtlest / most subtle |
| sure | surer / more sure | surest / most sure |

## as … as – tak … jako (stejně)

> I am as old as Mary is.
>
> We are as clever as you are.
>
> The film is as interesting as the book was.

**Pronunciation:** as /əz/, strong form /æz/

## not so (as) … as – ne tak … jako

> He has not so much money as we have.
>
> Susan hasn't so much time as I have.

## Intenzita přídavných jmen ve 2. stupni

Intenzitu měníme nejčastěji výrazy:

| Výraz | Význam | Příklad |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **much** | mnohem | much younger, much more active |
| **a lot** | mnohem | a lot better |
| **a little / a bit** | trochu | a little bigger |
| **even** | ještě | even simpler |
| **far** | daleko | far more hard-working |
| **quite** | docela | quite good |
| **rather** | poněkud/dost | rather unhappy |
| **so** | tak | so kind |
| **too** | příliš | too poor |

## more, less, fewer + than

**S počitatelnými podst. jmény – more / fewer**

> Eloise has more children than Chantal.
>
> Chantal has fewer children than Eloise.
>
> There are fewer dogs in Cardiff than in Bristol.
>
> I have visited fewer countries than my friend has.
>
> He has read fewer books than she has.

**S nepočitatelnými podst. jmény – more / less**

> Eloise has more money than Chantal.
>
> Chantal has less money than Eloise.
>
> I spend less time on homework than you do.
>
> Cats drink less water than dogs.
>
> This new dictionary gives more information than the old one.

## as much as, as many as, as few as, as little as

**With countable nouns:**

> They have as many children as us.
>
> We have as many customers as them.
>
> Tom has as few books as Jane.
>
> You know as few words in Spanish as I do.

**With uncountable nouns:**

> John eats as much food as Peter.
>
> You've heard as much news as I have.
>
> Jim has as little food as Sam.
>
> They've got as little water as we have.

## Dva komparativy s and

When we want to describe **how something or someone changes** we can use two comparatives with **and**:

> The balloon got bigger and bigger.
>
> Everything is getting more and more expensive.
>
> Grandfather is looking older and older.

## The … the …

We often use **the** with comparative adjectives to show that **one thing depends on another**:

> The faster you drive, the more dangerous it is.
> (= When you drive faster, it is more dangerous.)
>
> The higher they climbed, the colder it got.
> (= When they climbed higher, it got colder.)

## too (příliš)

> I am too young to drink alcohol.
>
> The holiday is too expensive to buy.
>
> We live too far to walk to school.

## (not) enough – (ne) dost

**enough + podstatné jméno**

> I haven't got enough money to go to the cimena.
>
> There are enough chairs in that room.

**přídavné jméno + enough**

> It's not hot enough to go swimming.
>
> She is strong enough to lift it.

### Solution – too/enough

1. Jsem příliš unavený na to, abych šel na procházku. → I'm too tired to walk.
2. Můj syn je pořád ještě moc malý na to, aby jezdil na kole. → My son is still too small to ride a bike.
3. Nejsme dost bohatí na to, abychom cestovali každý rok. → We aren't rich enough to travel every year.
4. Jsi dost starý na to, abys věděl takové věci. → You are old enough to know such things.
5. Marie je moc líná na to, aby vařila pro svou rodinu. → Mary is too lazy to cook for her family.
6. Nejsem dost silný na to, abych zvedl ten balík. → I'm not strong enough to lift the packet.
