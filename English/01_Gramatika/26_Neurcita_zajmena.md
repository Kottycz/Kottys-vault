# 26 – Neurčitá zájmena (Indefinite pronouns)

## Přehled

| | some | any | no | every |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **věc** | something | anything | nothing | everything |
| **osoba** | somebody | anybody | nobody | everybody |
| **místo** | somewhere | anywhere | nowhere | everywhere |

## Použití

**some** – v **kladných oznamovacích větách, nabídkách a zdvořilých žádostech**.

> George has got something to say.
>
> Would you like something to drink?

**any** – v **záporných větách a zjišťovacích otázkách**. V kladné oznamovací větě – **„kdokoli, kdekoli, cokoli"**.

> I haven't got anything to eat.
>
> I would live anywhere in Europe.

**no** – mají **záporný význam**, proto sloveso ve větě musí být **kladné**.

> Nobody came to see me today.

**every** – sloveso po složeninách s *every* musí být ve **třetí osobě jednotného čísla** – is/was/works....

> Everybody is happy to know indefinite pronouns :-).

## Zájmena jako podmět

Pokud neurčitá zájmena plní funkci **podmětu**, používáme **slovesa v jednotném čísle** a **zájmena v čísle množném**.

> Everybody is doing **their** best.
>
> Everyone is here, aren't **they**?
>
> If anybody calls, tell **them** I'll come back soon.
>
> Everybody is happy when **they** win in a lottery.

## Cvičení

Zadání: [[26_Neurcita_zajmena_cviceni]]

### Solution – Translate

1. Anyone can see what's going on here. → Kdokoli může vidět, oč tu běží.
2. Everyone clapped when the movie ended. → Každý zatleskal, když film skončil.
3. Does anyone have something that could help me with anything? → Má někdo něco, co by mi pomohlo s čímkoli?
4. Everything is taken care of. → O vše je postaráno.
5. I really just needed some time away from everyone. → Já opravdu jen potřebuji nějaký čas být pryč ode všech.
6. My parents always told me that anything is possible. → Mí rodiče mi vždy říkali, že cokoli je možné.
7. I would live anywhere hot. It doesn't matter. → Žila bych kdekoliv, kde je horko. Je mi to jedno.
8. He just needs somewhere to stay. → Jen potřebuje někde žít.

### Solution – Put in indefinite pronouns

1. I didn't see Max at the gym. In fact, I didn't see **anybody**. The place was empty.
2. James, **somebody** is here to see you.
3. **Anybody** can play that game. It is very easy.
4. Peter didn't see **anything** suspicious.
5. Davis told **nobody** his secret. He is a mystery man.
6. I have **something** for you. Look.

## Příběh na závěr

This is a little story about four people named **Everybody, Somebody, Anybody, and Nobody**.

> There was an important job to be done and Everybody was sure that Somebody would do it.
>
> Anybody could have done it, but Nobody did it.
>
> Somebody got angry about that because it was Everybody's job.
>
> Everybody thought that Anybody could do it, but Nobody realized that Everybody wouldn't do it.
>
> It ended up that Everybody blamed Somebody when Nobody did what Anybody could have done.
