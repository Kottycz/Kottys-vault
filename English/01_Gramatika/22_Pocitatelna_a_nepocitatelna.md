# 22 – Počitatelná a nepočitatelná podstatná jména

**Počitatelná** podstatná jména (countable) **můžeme počítat**, lze u nich určit počet kusů.

U **nepočitatelných** podstatných jmen (uncountable) **nelze určit počet kusů**, nelze je dát do množného čísla.

Nepočitatelná podstatná jména jsou:

- **látky** (milk, water, toothpaste...)
- **abstraktní věci, myšlenky a nápady** (knowledge, love, happiness ...)

## Typicky nepočitatelná slova

- **Food, drinks and liquids:** cheese, bread, pasta, coffee, milk, petrol, fuel, etc.
- **Materials:** iron, wood, metal, paper, plastic, etc.
- **Abstract ideas and feelings:** information, advice, strength, time, love, excitement, etc.
- **Illnesses:** diabetes, alzheimer, cancer, etc.
- **Languages:** English, French, Spanish, etc.

**Uncountable in English but not in other languages:**

- advice, news (it ends in -s, but it's a singular word)
- furniture, luggage, baggage, bread, cheese, toast, etc.

## Pozor!

Tato podstatná jména jsou také **nepočitatelná**:

> homework, knowledge, information, news, advice, furniture (nábytek), luggage (zavazadlo), traffic, scenery, pollution, bread, money, hair ....

Tato podstatná jména jsou naopak **počitatelná** a často v množném čísle:

> sky – skies

Ve slovníku nalezneme u podstatných jmen zkratky **C/U**.

## Countable and uncountable zároveň

Some nouns can be countable and uncountable because they can refer to a **unit** or to **'mass' or 'material'**. Compare:

> Yesterday I had two coffees. (= two cups of coffee)
>
> I love coffee. (= the liquid that we drink)

> I found two hairs in my soup. (two single hairs)
>
> She has beautiful hair. (= the mass of hair on her head)

## Jak z nepočitatelného udělat počitatelné

To make an uncountable noun into a countable one, we need to use a **container**. This is an expression which usually looks like: **a… of…**

For example: a cup of, a bottle of, a loaf of …..

| Nepočitatelné | S jednotkou |
| :-- | :-- |
| milk | a bottle / a litre / a glass of milk |
| advice | 3 pieces of advice / a bit of advice |
| homework | 2 pieces of homework |
| knowledge | one item of knowledge |

> Example: dva domácí úkoly – two pieces of homework

## Both countable and uncountable

Some nouns, such as **fish** and **chicken**, can be both countable and uncountable. It depends on whether you are referring to the **animal**, which is countable, or the **food**, which is uncountable.

> I've never liked chickens. They have crazy eyes.
>
> It was a feast! I'd never seen so much chicken! (kuřecí maso)

> We caught three fishes today.
>
> The restaurant had leftovers so I grabbed as much fish as I could carry!

## Změna významu

Někdy může být podstatné jméno jak počitatelné, tak nepočitatelné, ale **změní se jeho význam**.

| Countable | Slovo | Uncountable |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| There are two hairs in my coffee! | hair | I don't have much hair. |
| There are two lights in our bedroom. | light | Close the curtain. There's too much light! |
| Shhhhh! I thought I heard a noise. / There are so many different noises in the city. | noise (zvuk × hluk) | It's difficult to work when there is so much noise. |
| Have you got a paper to read? (newspaper) / Hand me those student papers. | paper | I want to draw a picture. Have you got some paper? |
| Our house has seven rooms. | room | Is there room for me to sit here? |
| We had a great time at the party. / How many times have I told you no? | time | Have you got time for a cup of coffee? |
| Macbeth is one of Shakespeare's greatest works. | work | I have no money. I need work! |

**Drinks** (coffee, water, orange juice) are usually uncountable. But if we are thinking of a cup or a glass, we can say (in a restaurant, for example):

> Two teas and one coffee please.

### -f, -fe → -ves

Some nouns ending with **f, fe**, drop this ending and add **ves** to make the plural form.

> a knife – knives
>
> a wife – two wives
>
> one half – two halves
>
> my life – their lives
>
> a wolf – wolves

### Stejný tvar

The following animals have the same plural form as the singular form:

> sheep – sheep, deer – deer, moose – moose, fish – fish
