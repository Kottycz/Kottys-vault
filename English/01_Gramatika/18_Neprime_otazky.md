# 18 – Nepřímé otázky

Unit 8E – Reported speech – nepřímá řeč v otázce

## Slovosled

V nepřímé otázce je v angličtině **stejný slovosled jako v oznamovací větě** – nejdřív **podmět**, potom **sloveso**. Není tedy zapotřebí používat pomocných sloves (**DO, DOES, DID**).

> "What is the time?"
>
> She wanted to know what the time was.

> "Where does David work?"
>
> She asked me where David worked.

## Posun časů

K posunu časů a změnám zájmen dochází **jako u nepřímé řeči v oznamovací větě** (viz [[backshift]]).

> "Why hasn't he come?"
>
> They wondered why he hadn't come.

> "How old are you?"
>
> They asked me how old I was.

## if / whether

Tam, kde otázka **nezačíná tázacím zájmenem** (where, why, when, how long ....), je nutné použít spojku **if / whether = jestli**.

> "Do you want a ride?" → Mike asked me **if** I wanted a ride.
>
> "Are you coming?" → They wanted to know **if** I was coming.
>
> "Will you be here later?" → She asked me **whether** I would be here later.

## Cvičení

Zadání: [[18_Neprime_otazky_cviceni]]

### Solution – Report the questions

1. We asked our mum **if/whether Uncle Matt and his girlfriend were getting married in July**.
2. I asked Darren **what songs his band were performing at the concert on Friday**.
3. Mandy asked me **if/whether I had bought anything in town**.
4. The boys asked us **if/whether we had ever tried rock climbing**.
5. We asked the teacher **who had won the school science competition**.

### Practise – yes/no questions

| Přímá otázka | Nepřímá otázka |
| :-- | :-- |
| "Do you know London?" | She asked me if I knew London. |
| "Did he leave for work?" | He asked me if he had left for work. |
| "Have you been to Madagaskar?" | They questioned me if I had been to Madagascar. |
| "Can you help me?" | She asked me if I could help her. |
| "Are you working tonight?" | She asked me if I was working that night. |

### Practise – "wh" questions

| Přímá otázka | Nepřímá otázka |
| :-- | :-- |
| "Where is Sam?" | She asked me where Sam was. |
| "What were you doing?" | She asked me what I had been doing. |
| "Why did you stay at home last night?" | She wanted to know why I had stayed at home the night before. |
| "Who wants to play soccer?" | He wondered who wanted to play soccer. |
| "How is your mother?" | They asked me how my mother was. |
