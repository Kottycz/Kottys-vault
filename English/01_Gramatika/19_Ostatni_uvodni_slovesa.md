# 19 – Ostatní úvodní slovesa (reporting verbs)

Unit 8E

## Say or tell?

**Say** i **tell** překládáme jako **říct**. Pokud použijeme **tell**, uvádíme po tomto slovese **ihned komu** – jim, nám, jemu atd.

Po **say** stává spíše **„that"** nebo komu s předložkou **to** – to us, to him atd.

> She **told me/you/him/her/us/them** that she would call at 2pm.
>
> She **said that** it was raining. / She **said to us** that it was raining.

### Cvičení

Zadání: [[19_Ostatni_uvodni_slovesa_cviceni]]

**Solution:**

1. He **told** everybody that he had to do it.
2. Alice **told** us that she was going to bed.
3. "We had never paid a fine for speeding", they **said** to us.
4. Jordan **said** that he was tired.
5. "I've already seen this film", Mark **said** to me.
6. "This joke made me laugh", Susan **said** to me.

## Other reporting verbs

Slovesa, která slouží k vyjádření nepřímé řeči:

> accused, admitted, advised, agreed, apologised, begged, boasted, complained, confessed, explained, invited, offered, ordered, promised, replied, suggested and thought ...........

> He invited me to the party.
>
> He begged me to come to the party.
>
> He ordered me to come to the party.
>
> He advised me to come to the party.
>
> He suggested I should come to the party.

## Tři vazby po úvodních slovesech

### 1) sloveso + that

**said, told, pointed out, explained, wrote, complained, agreed, claim, repeat, mention, insist, state**

Po těchto slovesech můžeme použít vedlejší větu uvozenou spojkou **THAT**. Tato se často v hovorové angličtině **vynechává**.

> She answered (that) she had never wanted to study biology.

### 2) sloveso + infinitiv

**admitted, agreed, confessed, offered, ordered, promised, suggested, refused ....**

Má-li hlavní i vedlejší věta **stejný podmět**, můžeme po těchto slovesech vedlejší větu zkrátit pomocí **infinitivu**.

> He promised to come home early.

### 3) sloveso + předmět + infinitiv

**asked, begged, warned, wanted, advised, promised, offered, threatened, encouraged, persuaded, reminded, assured, encouraged**

Po některých slovesech použijeme nejprve **předmět** (me/him/them...), a poté **infinitiv**.

> He promised us to come home early.

## Cvičení – Translate the sentences

Zadání: [[19_Ostatni_uvodni_slovesa_cviceni]]

**Řešení:**

| Přímá řeč | Nepřímá řeč |
| :-- | :-- |
| "Yes, you're right, it's a terrible problem." | She **agreed that** it was a terrible problem. |
| "I'm sorry I didn't get to the meeting." | He **apologised for** not going to the meeting. |
| "They didn't do their job properly." | She **complained that** they hadn't done their job properly. |
| "Sorry I'm late. The traffic was bad…" | He **explained that** the traffic had been bad. |
| "Those bags must be heavy, John. Shall I take one?" | She **offered to** carry a bag for him. |
| "Yes, honest, I'll be there on time. I won't be late." | He **promised that** he would be there on time. |

## Cvičení – Translate (z češtiny)

**Řešení:**

1. Řekl mi, abych to nedělal. → He told me not to do it.
2. Poradil jim, aby šli rychle pryč. → He advised them to go away quickly.
3. Povzbudil mě, abych se nebál. → He encouraged me not to be afraid.
4. Přesvědčila ho, aby tam jel. → She persuaded him to go there.
5. Připomněli mi, abych odevzdala tu práci. → They reminded me to hand in the work.
6. Chtěl po mě, abych se více učil. → He wanted me to learn more.
7. Požádali jsme je, aby nám o tom vyprávěli. → We asked them to tell us about it.

## Cvičení – Choose the correct words

**Solution:** 1 A, 2 B, 3 B, 4 A, 5 B

1. A man on the beach warned us **against** swimming in the sea because it was very stormy.
2. Roberta suggested **going** to the cinema on Saturday.
3. Dad reminded us **to phone** him when we arrived in London.
4. I insist on **paying** for this pizza. You bought me lunch last week.
5. Gary accused me **of** copying his homework.
